# AI reading experience and waiver rationale

The waiver briefing now asks Qwen for two short original sentences describing each candidate's case and limitation. This is labeled AI interpretation, separate from canonical projection/depth facts and cited reporting. The prompt forbids inventing news conclusions from titles, numerical forecasts and guarantees; output is bounded to 500 characters. Schema validation verifies player IDs, evidence keys and citation indices, not semantic truth of the AI's interpretation. Users should review the linked evidence.

Three Draft Sharks RSS feeds (player news/Shark Bites, injury news and advice) join ESPN, Yahoo, CBS and FantasyPros plus cached player news searches. Feeds use the existing hourly cache, seven-day article window and stale/unavailable labels. Yahoo scraping scope is unchanged.

The portal now bundles Inter locally for consistent typography, including form controls and charts. AI briefs use a shared card layout with distinct priority, assessment and evidence areas; sources are expandable to reduce clutter. Team priorities and the full assessment are separated. Waiver and team insight charts show player projections in Qwen's review order. A decision-flow diagram explains inputs, assessment and user review. Chart values are also available as text. These charts do not assert confidence, agreement or win probability.

Research version 5 regenerates the latest briefing on rollout. Validation includes bounded assessment tests, existing evidence rejection tests, production build and browser inspection of both AI pages.
